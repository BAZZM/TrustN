# GUI & experience – your input

Use this to shape how the Trust Network looks and behaves on **website** and **phone**. Your answers will guide the next design and feature pass.

---

## 1. Overall feel

- What **mood** should the app have? (e.g. calm and minimal, energetic, professional, playful, serious/formal.)
- Any **reference apps or sites** you like (for layout, motion, or tone)?
- Prefer **dark**, **light**, or **system (auto)** as the default theme?

---

## 2. Web vs phone

- Should the **website** feel like a full desktop experience (e.g. sidebar, multiple columns) or more like a focused, single-column flow similar to the phone?
- On **phone**, do you want a **bottom tab bar** (like now), **top tabs**, **hamburger menu**, or a mix (e.g. bottom nav + slide-out menu)?
- Any actions that should be **different** on phone vs web? (e.g. “Add connection” only on desktop, or always in the same place.)

---

## 3. Motion & animation

- How much motion do you want? **Minimal** (only essential feedback), **medium** (smooth transitions and light feedback), or **rich** (more decorative motion)?
- Any **animations you dislike**? (e.g. things sliding from the side, bouncy springs, long page transitions.)
- Should **loading** states be: skeleton screens, spinners, progress bars, or “quiet” (no visible loader)?

---

## 4. Trust Network–specific

- What are the **main actions** users do most? (e.g. view connections, add/remove trust, see activity, manage profile.) Which should be **one tap away** on phone?
- How should **trust/connection strength** be shown? (e.g. numbers, levels, colors, simple on/off.)
- Do you want **notifications** (in-app, push, or both)? Any preference for how they look (banners, toasts, center modals)?

---

## 5. Accessibility & preferences

- Do you need **large text** or **high contrast** as an option?
- Any **language or locale** requirements (e.g. RTL, multiple languages)?
- Should we **respect “reduce motion”** everywhere (we already do for key animations) or are there places where motion is essential?

---

## 6. Anything else

- Screens or flows you **don’t want** (e.g. no carousels, no full-screen modals).
- Things you **really want** (e.g. pull-to-refresh on phone, keyboard shortcuts on web).
- Copy tone: more **friendly**, **neutral**, or **formal**?

---

*Reply in this doc, in a separate file, or in chat – we’ll use it to refine the GUI for both the website and the phone app.*
